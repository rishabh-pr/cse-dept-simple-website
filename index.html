About Us

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering was started in 1998. We teach students programming, data structures, databases, computer networks and many other subjects that are needed to become a good software engineer. Our teachers and students also take part in coding competitions, workshops and college events every year.

Why Study With Us

Good Labs

We have computer labs with fast internet and updated software for practicals.

Friendly Teachers

Our teachers are always ready to help students with doubts and projects.

Placements

Many students from our department get placed in good companies every year.
